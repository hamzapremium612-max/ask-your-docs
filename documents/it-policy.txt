NORTHFIELD TRADING CO. — IT & ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

PERSONAL USE OF COMPANY DEVICES
Limited personal use of company laptops and phones is permitted outside working
hours, provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive bandwidth, or
breach any section of this policy. Personal files stored on company devices are
not backed up and may be erased without notice during maintenance.

PROHIBITED ACTIVITY
Company devices and networks must not be used to access illegal content, install
unlicensed software, run cryptocurrency mining, or operate a personal business.
Streaming video during core working hours is discouraged as it degrades network
performance for the whole office.

PASSWORDS AND ACCOUNTS
All accounts require a password of at least 14 characters, changed every 180
days. Passwords must never be shared, written down, or reused across services.
Two factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, and any system
holding customer data. Report a suspected compromise to IT within one hour.

SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
Employees may install software from the approved catalogue without permission.
Anything outside the catalogue requires written IT approval before installation.
Browser extensions count as software and follow the same rule.

DATA HANDLING
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage,
or emailed to a personal address. Files containing customer records must stay
within the company Google Workspace. Breaches of this section are treated as
gross misconduct.

REMOTE ACCESS AND VPN
Staff working remotely must connect through the company VPN before accessing
internal systems. Public wifi may only be used with the VPN active. The VPN
must not be used to bypass regional content restrictions.

DEVICE SECURITY
Devices must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Laptops must
not be left unattended in vehicles or public spaces. A lost or stolen device
must be reported to IT immediately so it can be wiped remotely.

EMAIL AND PHISHING
Treat unexpected attachments and payment requests as suspicious, even from known
senders. Never approve a payment based on email instruction alone; confirm by
phone using a number you already hold. Forward suspected phishing to
security@northfield.example and then delete it.
