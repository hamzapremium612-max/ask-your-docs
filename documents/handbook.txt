NORTHFIELD TRADING CO. — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

WORKING HOURS
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one hour
unpaid lunch break. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all staff
are expected to be reachable. Outside core hours, staff may adjust their start
and finish times by up to ninety minutes with their manager's approval.

ANNUAL VACATION ENTITLEMENT
Full time employees receive 22 working days of paid vacation per calendar year,
in addition to public holidays. Entitlement increases to 25 days after three
completed years of service, and to 28 days after seven years. Vacation must be
requested at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. A maximum of
five unused days may be carried into the following year; anything beyond that
is forfeited on 31 December.

SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than three consecutive working days. Sick days
do not carry over and are not paid out on departure. Notify your manager before
10:00 AM on the first day of absence.

REMOTE WORK
Staff may work remotely up to two days per week after completing probation.
Requests are approved by the line manager and reviewed quarterly. Fully remote
arrangements are considered case by case and require director approval. Remote
staff must maintain a reliable internet connection and attend all scheduled
video meetings with camera on.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission. Receipts are
mandatory for every claim regardless of amount. Claims must be submitted within
60 days of the expense being incurred; late claims are rejected. Meals during
business travel are capped at 4,000 rupees per day. Air travel is economy class
only unless the flight exceeds eight hours.

COMPANY EQUIPMENT
Each employee is issued a laptop and, where the role requires it, a mobile
phone. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on the final day
of employment. Loss or damage through negligence may be deducted from final
salary. Personal use of company equipment is permitted within reason but is
subject to the IT acceptable use policy.

PROBATION PERIOD
New employees serve a probation period of three months. During probation either
party may terminate the employment with one week of notice. Probation may be
extended once by a further three months where performance requires review.
Confirmation of employment is issued in writing by HR.

NOTICE PERIOD
After probation, employees must give one calendar month of written notice. Staff
at manager level and above must give two calendar months. The company reserves
the right to place departing employees on garden leave for all or part of the
notice period.

TRAINING BUDGET
Each employee has an annual training budget of 60,000 rupees for courses,
conferences, and books relevant to their role. Approval is required from the
line manager before booking. Unused budget does not carry into the next year.
